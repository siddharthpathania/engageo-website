A familiar scene plays out daily in busy Indian orthopaedic outpatient departments (OPD). A doctor evaluates an older patient, identifies a risk for osteoporosis, and advises a dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) scan. The doctor writes the test on the paper slip and asks the patient to book it at the desk. Yet many patients walk out of the clinic without scheduling their test.

The patient walks into a crowded waiting room. People are queuing for billing, walk-in patients are asking about token numbers, and the phone rings without stopping. Rather than waiting another twenty minutes to check scanner slots, the patient puts the prescription slip into their bag. They plan to call back later. For many clinics, that call never arrives.

This drop-off harms patient outcomes and lowers diagnostic clinic revenue. When an in-house bone densitometer sits idle while the OPD is full, the bottleneck is front-desk bandwidth. By automating scan bookings, pre-test preparation alerts, and review reminders, orthopaedic practices can eliminate delays and complete more tests.

## DEXA Scan Appointment Scheduling: Closing the OPD Referral Gap

Most private clinics in India leave diagnostic bookings entirely to the patient. A patient must carry a paper slip to the desk, check machine availability, and pay an advance. When the receptionist is busy with registrations and billing queues, this handoff easily breaks down.

Orthopaedic practices can stop this drop-off by borrowing proven care-coordination methods. In [Fracture Liaison Service (FLS) programmes](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/), structured workflows operate under a doctor and an FLS coordinator. They help ensure patients who have sustained fractures receive the diagnostic assessment, treatment, and follow-ups needed to lower future fracture risk ([Fracture Liaison Service "FLS"](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/)). Under this model, coordinators contact patients aged 50 or older who have sustained a broken bone ([Fracture Liaison Service "FLS"](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/)).

Care coordination led by an FLS coordinator also helps make sure patients keep their follow-up medical appointments and stay on prescription medications designed to prevent fractures ([Fracture Liaison Service "FLS"](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/)). Independent orthopaedic clinics can replicate these steps with software. Instead of relying on paper slips, clinics can trigger an automated booking message sent to the patient's phone the moment the doctor recommends a scan.

This automated workflow should connect scanner openings directly with doctor consultation hours. Pairing the scanner calendar with doctor OPD schedules creates two convenient patient options:

- Same-Day Testing: If the scanner has open slots within an hour of the consultation, the system offers an immediate booking. The patient finishes testing and billing in one trip.
- Matched Return Visits: If the scanner is busy, the workflow suggests slots during the doctor's next OPD session. The patient can then do the scan and review their results in a single return visit.

## Automating Pre-Scan Preparation Rules to Prevent Rescheduled Slots

Booking the slot is only the first step. DEXA scanners require strict patient preparation. When patients do not prepare properly, clinics face cancelled slots, lost table time, and repeated scans. Maintaining consistent patient preparation, including clothing choice, is essential for obtaining [precise bone mineral density measures](https://iscd.org/official-positions-2023/) ([Official Adult Positions - ISCD](https://iscd.org/official-positions-2023/)).

Receptionists rarely have time to explain every preparation rule during a hectic morning rush. Automated messages sent before the visit solve the three most common preparation issues:

### 1\. The 24-Hour Calcium Supplement Pause

Patients preparing for a bone density test must [refrain from taking calcium supplements](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/dexa) for at least 24 hours prior to the procedure ([Bone Density Scan (DEXA or DXA)](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/dexa), [Bone density test (DXA/DEXA scan)](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/bone-density-test/about/pac-20385273)). Many orthopaedic patients take daily calcium tablets. An undissolved pill sitting in the stomach during a spine scan can alter density readings. Clinics should schedule an automated alert 24 hours before the appointment reminding the patient to skip their calcium tablets that day.

### 2\. Contrast Material and Barium Test Screening

The recent administration of contrast material and artifacts are recognized limitations in the clinical use of bone density scans ([Official Adult Positions - ISCD](https://iscd.org/official-positions-2023/)). Patients who recently underwent a barium examination or received contrast injections for a CT or radioisotope scan may need to wait 10 to 14 days before undergoing a DEXA test ([Bone Density Scan (DEXA or DXA)](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/dexa)).

Patients should let their healthcare team know if they have recently received contrast for a CT scan, a nuclear medicine test, or had a barium examination, because these can [interfere with the test](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/bone-density-test/about/pac-20385273) ([Bone density test (DXA/DEXA scan)](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/bone-density-test/about/pac-20385273)). Automated pre-screening messages can ask patients about recent scans and hold bookings until the safe window passes.

### 3\. Clothing and Metal Guidelines

Patients preparing for a DEXA scan should wear loose, comfortable clothing without metal fasteners like zippers, buttons, or belts ([Bone Density Scan (DEXA or DXA)](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/dexa), [Bone density test (DXA/DEXA scan)](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/bone-density-test/about/pac-20385273)). They should leave jewellery at home and take out metal items such as coins and keys from their pockets ([Bone density test (DXA/DEXA scan)](https://www.mayoclinic.org/tests-procedures/bone-density-test/about/pac-20385273)). Patients may also need to change into an examination gown ([Bone Density Scan (DEXA or DXA)](https://www.radiologyinfo.org/en/info/dexa)). Artifacts represent a recognized clinical limitation during testing ([Official Adult Positions - ISCD](https://iscd.org/official-positions-2023/)). Sending clothing instructions three hours before the test helps patients arrive ready and cuts changing room delays.

## Overcoming Booking Hurdles for Elderly Patients with Multilingual Voice and Messaging

DEXA scans are often prescribed to senior citizens and post-menopausal women. This group faces clear communication challenges at busy Indian clinics. Older patients rarely download mobile apps, and fast English phone menus often cause them to hang up.

To reduce no-shows, clinics must communicate the way senior patients do. Simple voice calls or messages in regional languages—like Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, or Tamil—build quick trust. When an older patient hears a clear voice note in their own language explaining the scan time, they confirm their appointment much faster than they would with a text link in English.

Family members also manage care for many senior patients. Clinic workflows should collect contact details for an adult son, daughter, or caregiver. Sending duplicate confirmations, clinic location pins, and prep instructions to the caregiver's WhatsApp ensures the patient arrives on time and properly prepared.

## Capturing Inbound Scan Inquiries with Missed Call Recovery and AI Receptionists

Unanswered phone calls cause major revenue leaks. During peak OPD hours—usually 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 6:00 PM to 9:00 PM IST—receptionists must register walk-ins, collect consult fees, and manage lines. As a result, incoming phone calls often go unanswered.

Patients who call to check scan fees or book appointments rarely call back if the line is busy. They usually call another diagnostic centre nearby. Using missed call recovery tools allows clinics to capture these inquiries right away.

When the front desk cannot pick up an incoming call, an automated missed call text back can reply within seconds. It sends the patient a WhatsApp message with booking links, machine timings, and fee details. For callers who prefer to speak, an AI receptionist can answer overflow calls in Indian languages around the clock. The system answers basic questions and logs the booking into the clinic calendar.

## Automating the Post-Scan Review Consultation Loop

A DEXA scan is a diagnostic test, not the final treatment. Yet clinics often lose contact with patients once the scan is over. Patients collect their printed reports, look at unfamiliar T-score and Z-score numbers, and fail to book a review consult with the orthopaedic doctor.

Structured post-fracture care models show the value of ongoing care coordination. FLS coordinators provide patient education and counselling on fall prevention, safety measures, diet, calcium and vitamin D supplementation, exercise, and lifestyle changes like reducing alcohol or stopping smoking ([Fracture Liaison Service "FLS"](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/)).

These coordinators also ensure patients complete rehabilitation plans, keep follow-up medical appointments, and start and stay on prescription medications to prevent fractures ([Fracture Liaison Service "FLS"](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/)). In an FLS model, follow-up visits are scheduled one to three months after the initial evaluation to assess treatment and plan ongoing care ([Fracture Liaison Service "FLS"](https://www.bonehealthandosteoporosis.org/patients/communication-with-your-doctor/fracture-liaison-service-fls/)).

Clinics can automate this follow-up step. As soon as the radiologist signs off on the bone density report, the clinic can send a digital copy via WhatsApp with a clear prompt: Dr. Sharma has open OPD slots this week to review your scan and outline your treatment plan. Tap here to select your review time.

Closing this loop protects patient bone health and secures follow-up consultation revenue that clinics would otherwise lose to patient inaction.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do patients need to stop calcium supplements before a DEXA scan?

Patients must avoid taking calcium supplements for at least 24 hours prior to the scan. An undissolved pill sitting in the stomach during a spine evaluation can alter the density readings.

### How do contrast materials or barium tests affect DEXA scan scheduling?

Recent barium exams or contrast injections for CT and radioisotope scans can interfere with bone density readings and cause artifacts. Patients who recently had these procedures may need to wait 10 to 14 days before undergoing a DEXA scan.

### What clothing rules should patients follow for a bone density test?

Patients should wear loose, comfortable clothing free of metal fasteners such as zippers, buttons, or belts. They should also remove jewelry and empty metal items like keys and coins from their pockets to prevent testing artifacts.

### How can clinics prevent missed appointments among elderly patients?

Clinics can use simple voice calls or messages in regional languages to build trust, as well as send duplicate booking confirmations and prep details directly to adult caregivers or family members via WhatsApp.
