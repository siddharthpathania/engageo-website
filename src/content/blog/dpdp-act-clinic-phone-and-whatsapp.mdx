Someone calls a dental clinic at 9:42 PM. Nobody picks up. Next morning the front desk sees the missed call, saves the number, sends a WhatsApp message offering a slot, and adds the number to the festive-offer list. Three of those steps are ordinary clinic work. The fourth turns a patient into a complainant.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 received assent on 11 August 2023 ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)), and the Rules that make it work were notified on 14 November 2025 ([DPDP Rules, 2025 Notified (Press Information Bureau)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)). The rules on notice, security, breach reporting and retention come into force eighteen months after publication in the Gazette ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)), which puts the deadline in May 2027. Enough time to do this calmly. Not enough to ignore it.

What follows covers the three things a clinic does with a phone: answer it, record it, message afterwards. None of it is legal advice; have a lawyer read your consent form and vendor contracts.

## Your clinic is a data fiduciary

A Data Fiduciary decides why and how personal data is processed, a Data Principal is the individual it belongs to, and a Data Processor handles it on the fiduciary's behalf ([DPDP Rules, 2025 Notified (Press Information Bureau)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)). Your clinic is the fiduciary; your answering service or WhatsApp provider is a processor.

The Act covers digital personal data, including data collected on paper and digitised later ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)): a handwritten slip sits outside it until it is typed into your practice software.

Processing rests on one of two footings: consent, or certain legitimate uses ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). The one that matters at a front desk is section 7(a): the specified purpose for which a person voluntarily gave you her data, and has not said she objects to. The Act illustrates it with a pharmacy sending a payment receipt the customer asked for ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

So a patient who rings to book has handed you a number for booking. Calling her back about that appointment is fine. Using it three weeks later for a whitening offer is a different purpose, and that needs consent.

## Notice and consent at the registration desk

Under the Rules the notice must stand on its own, understandable independently of anything else you hand the patient, so a clause buried in the consent-to-treatment form does not qualify. It must give, in plain language, an itemised description of the data and the specified purposes, and say how to withdraw consent, exercise rights and complain to the Data Protection Board ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).

Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given by clear affirmative action, and limited to the data necessary for the purpose ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). The Act illustrates this with a telemedicine app that also asks for the phone's contact list: since the list is not necessary, consent is read down to exclude it ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Ask for a spouse's number and an email when one mobile will do, and the extra fields are not covered.

Two details get missed. The notice must be available in English or any language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)): if your waiting room speaks Marathi, it should exist in Marathi. And withdrawal must be as easy as consent was to give ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)): a tick on a tablet cannot require a signed letter to undo.

In practice this is one separate half-page at registration. What you collect: name, mobile, age, reason for visit, appointment history, call recordings. What for: booking, reminders, follow-up, billing. Then one optional line, ticked separately, for offers.

## Call recordings, and a clock that runs both ways

A recorded patient call is digital personal data, and two duties pull against each other.

You must erase: when consent is withdrawn, or as soon as it is reasonable to assume the specified purpose is no longer served, whichever is earlier, unless another law requires retention. You must also make your processor erase what you gave it ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

You must also retain. The Rules require personal data, associated traffic data and processing logs to be kept for at least one year from the date of processing, unless a law requires longer ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). The three-year auto-deletion in the Third Schedule targets named classes such as very large e-commerce platforms, not clinics ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).

The security floor is specific: encryption, obfuscation, masking or virtual tokens; access control; logs and monitoring; backups; and a contract term binding your processor to the same ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). In clinic terms, the recording archive is not a shared drive folder four receptionists and one former employee can still open.

One habit worth starting now: announce at the top of the call that it is being recorded and why, in the caller's language. On a channel with no screen, that is how "informed" happens.

## A reminder and an advertisement are different things

Meta requires opt-in before a business messages anyone on WhatsApp. It must clearly state that the person is opting in to receive messages, and name the business she will hear from. It can be collected over SMS, on a website, through an IVR, in person or on paper, so long as the method complies with applicable law ([Get opt-in for WhatsApp, Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in)). A tick box that names your clinic and says she will receive messages from it meets that bar. A number from a missed call is not.

Your SMS and voice outreach answers to a second regulator. TRAI requires an opt-out inside every promotional message, and treats promotional content mixed into a service or transactional message as making the whole message promotional ([Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference (Second Amend…](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf)). Service messages about something the customer already uses need no explicit consent, and transactional messages are those sent within thirty minutes of a customer-initiated transaction ([Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference (Second Amend…](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf)).

So this is a reminder: "Your appointment with Dr Mehta is Tuesday 11:30 AM. Reply 1 to confirm." Add "and ask about this month's scaling offer" and the whole message becomes advertising. Keep reminders clean and send offers separately, to the people who ticked the box.

## Your answering service is a processor, not a shield

The Act is blunt: you are responsible irrespective of any agreement to the contrary for processing done on your behalf, and may engage a processor only under a valid contract ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)) that provides for reasonable security safeguards ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). Whether the tool answering your number is Engageo or anything else, the questions are the same: where does the audio live, who can play it back, how long is it kept, what happens the day you switch it off.

Get four things in writing. The personal data the vendor touches. Retention periods for recordings, transcripts and message logs. Deletion on termination, since you must cause your processor to erase ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). And a breach clause with real timelines: the Rules require you to tell each affected patient without delay what happened and whom to contact, and to give the Board detailed information within seventy-two hours ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).

The numbers are not symbolic: up to 250 crore rupees for failing to maintain reasonable security safeguards, 200 crore for failing to notify a breach, and 50 crore for anything else ([DPDP Rules, 2025 Notified (Press Information Bureau)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)).

## Children, and the carve-out clinics get

A child is anyone under eighteen. As a rule, verifiable parental consent comes first, and tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising aimed at children are barred ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). The Fourth Schedule puts clinical establishments and healthcare professionals outside both obligations, on one condition: processing must be restricted to providing health services to the child, to the extent necessary for the protection of her health ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).

Read the condition, not the headline. Booking a fourteen-year-old's orthodontic review is covered. Adding her mother's number to a braces broadcast list is not health services.

## What to write down before May 2027

1. A register of what you collect at the desk, on the phone and over WhatsApp, and why.
2. The standalone notice, in English and your patients' language, with the data list and three routes out ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).
3. A retention schedule with a number against every item, call recordings included ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).
4. Consent records showing what was ticked and when, kept apart from the clinical record.
5. A named person, contact published, who answers data questions and replies to grievances within ninety days ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).
6. A breach runbook that starts the seventy-two-hour clock the moment someone notices ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).
7. A data annexure in every vendor contract: data, retention, deletion on exit, breach notice ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

None of it needs new software. It needs a morning and a decision about what you will stop collecting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do we need signed consent before calling a patient back about the appointment she rang for?

No. She gave the number for that purpose, which is a legitimate use ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Consent becomes necessary when you use it for something else.

### Can we keep call recordings indefinitely, just in case?

No. You must erase once the purpose is served or consent is withdrawn, unless a law requires retention ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Nor can you delete at once: the Rules set a one-year minimum ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). Pick a defensible period above that floor and write it down.

### Is a WhatsApp appointment reminder a promotional message?

Not if it carries only appointment information. Mix an offer in and, on your telecom channels, TRAI's definitions make the whole message promotional ([Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference (Second Amend…](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf)). Meta separately requires opt-in naming your business before you message at all ([Get opt-in for WhatsApp, Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in)).

### What if our messaging vendor leaks a patient list?

You stay responsible, irrespective of any agreement to the contrary ([The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (No. 22 of 2023)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Tell each affected patient without delay, and give the Board detailed information within seventy-two hours ([Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (Gazette of India, Ext…](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). Failure to notify can attract up to 200 crore rupees ([DPDP Rules, 2025 Notified (Press Information Bureau)](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf)).
