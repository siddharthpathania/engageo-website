## Script 1: Active OPD Hours Missed Call Text Back Script Template

During peak morning OPD and evening clinic hours, front desks experience acute call congestion. Receptionists are actively attending to patients standing at the counter, processing diagnostic reports, and managing doctor consultations. Picking up a phone call while handling an in-person patient creates friction at the desk, but letting the call ring out drives the caller to another provider. Research indicates that [healthcare text messages experience an average response rate of 53.09%](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHq6fFZf0ckh2Aan8Cuh5MHGKjD2qzAxF-_yYLTh34dkjC8gIwPbxVXu5ew_VL5mKBL-zfs19rawdcs1D41OZRiziRe62Czk5NEkX4gNwUQyzukyFw19Kp08gGMGk2QEX79cm_9p3zw4wA6ls6rAI08M8or6mv5DSvSKJE8ML8Alfg4XSph2O7yUpcI) ([SMS Benchmarks for Healthcare and Social Assistance](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHq6fFZf0ckh2Aan8Cuh5MHGKjD2qzAxF-_yYLTh34dkjC8gIwPbxVXu5ew_VL5mKBL-zfs19rawdcs1D41OZRiziRe62Czk5NEkX4gNwUQyzukyFw19Kp08gGMGk2QEX79cm_9p3zw4wA6ls6rAI08M8or6mv5DSvSKJE8ML8Alfg4XSph2O7yUpcI)). An immediate automated text-back sent within seconds reassures the patient that the practice is operational and provides a direct route to schedule an appointment.

### Peak OPD Queue Script Template

"Namaste \[Patient Name / Caller\], thank you for calling \[Clinic Name\]. Our front desk is currently assisting patients in the OPD queue and we missed your call. We want to ensure you are seen without delay. You can reserve your preferred doctor and consultation time directly using our digital desk here: \[Booking Link\]. If you prefer speaking to our team, reply 'CALL' to this message and our coordinator will phone you back as soon as the counter clears."

### Implementation Notes: Active OPD Response

- Trigger this message within 30 to 60 seconds of a missed call during defined OPD hours.
- Keep the phrasing respectful and transparent about counter queues, which resonates with Indian patients familiar with busy clinic waiting rooms.
- Ensure the booking link opens directly into available doctor consultation slots rather than a generic homepage.
- If the patient replies 'CALL', ensure the receptionist desk ledger flags the contact for a callback within 30 minutes.

## Script 2: After-Hours and Weekend Slot Selection

A substantial portion of healthcare search queries and appointment attempts happen outside standard clinic operating hours. According to platform data from Zocdoc, [45% of medical bookings are scheduled outside regular clinic operational hours](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHv0S1z_TY2PdiZdo1jqBJlgXYOWctORVhyrja9u9ORCcfCWSQ8ulJ2heMl2kxwehnp5NIXNQPsoDndp9tfXiJYVuArB5lune6dk6-Vf3xdMhCdaOesaPOxuo-aWq3CrwGujxLU9HJanFGYbGHctNmzzItRWw==) ([Missed Call Statistics for Medical Offices (2026)](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHv0S1z_TY2PdiZdo1jqBJlgXYOWctORVhyrja9u9ORCcfCWSQ8ulJ2heMl2kxwehnp5NIXNQPsoDndp9tfXiJYVuArB5lune6dk6-Vf3xdMhCdaOesaPOxuo-aWq3CrwGujxLU9HJanFGYbGHctNmzzItRWw==)). When a patient experiences symptoms late in the evening or realizes on a Sunday afternoon that they need a specialist consultation, they call the practice immediately. If the call disconnects without information, the patient assumes the practice cannot assist them and turns to 24-hour hospital outpatient networks.

An after-hours text-back captures this high-intent demand. It clarifies your operating hours, provides immediate calendar access, and gives the patient certainty that their next-day or upcoming appointment slot is confirmed.

### After-Hours & Weekend Script Template

"Hello, thank you for contacting \[Clinic Name\]. Our clinic reception is currently closed. Our regular OPD hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST. Although our desk is closed tonight, our appointment schedule is open 24/7. You can view upcoming open slots for Dr. \[Doctor Name\] and reserve your consultation for tomorrow or later this week here: \[Booking Link\]. Your slot will be logged immediately in our register, and our front desk will verify your arrival details at opening."

### Implementation Notes: After-Hours Routing

- Configure the trigger window to run automatically from clinic closing time until 30 minutes prior to morning opening, as well as all day on practice holidays.
- State your operational hours clearly in Indian Standard Time (IST) so patient expectations match your clinic routine.
- Direct the link to live calendar slots to prevent double-booking morning walk-ins.

## Script 3: Emergency Triage and In-Person Escalation Script

Furthermore, in any medical emergency, guidelines require that patients be advised to seek an in-person consultation with a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) at the earliest opportunity ([Guidelines for Practice Released by Ministry of Health - Telemedici…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHTtuwCE8K2UytxkN1JtHG5Gwf1Pn_V0W67S0eIx5GAzFLO73oGsAeJy-RP8XpVC5YjbnJ91qjoHl1aAoL1Q7OczQj3QCyKGNCEEz3lCHT01fOWnDnbDaRle6SUrbfUUDV95bAa2olnfShM548LSdoUuXEgz2mVr--QNzMgWKme)). A compliant triage text-back must establish firm clinical boundaries: it should instruct callers experiencing acute emergencies to visit an emergency facility immediately, while directing non-emergency patients to routine scheduling.

### Emergency Triage Script Template

"Thank you for calling \[Clinic Name\]. If you or the caller are experiencing an acute medical emergency—such as chest pain, severe breathlessness, profuse bleeding, or acute trauma—please do not wait for a callback. Head immediately to the nearest hospital emergency room or casualty ward, or contact emergency ambulance services. If you are calling for a planned consultation, follow-up visit, or routine health check-up with our Registered Medical Practitioners, please select your appointment time here: \[Booking Link\]. Our clinical desk does not provide diagnoses or prescriptions over automated messages."

### Implementation Notes: Emergency Escalation

- Place emergency warnings at the top of the message so critical callers do not spend time reading appointment options.
- Advise callers on immediate local hospital casualty attendance alongside emergency ambulance contacts.
- Do not use automated messaging systems to counsel patients or prescribe medications, adhering strictly to Ministry of Health telemedicine rules ([Guidelines for Practice Released by Ministry of Health - Telemedici…](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHTtuwCE8K2UytxkN1JtHG5Gwf1Pn_V0W67S0eIx5GAzFLO73oGsAeJy-RP8XpVC5YjbnJ91qjoHl1aAoL1Q7OczQj3QCyKGNCEEz3lCHT01fOWnDnbDaRle6SUrbfUUDV95bAa2olnfShM548LSdoUuXEgz2mVr--QNzMgWKme)).

## Script 4: Multi-Speciality Department Routing Template

Polyclinics and multi-speciality medical centres deal with a broader variety of inbound inquiries than solo practices. A caller might require an orthopaedic surgeon, a dermatologist, a gynaecologist, or a paediatrician. Unanswered call rates in speciality care sit at 36% ([The Cost of Missed Appointment Calls for Healthcare Marketers](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHNlkzw8gAhCKKbapeWHYjGNI3B9OWtkOdieEAfHHTyseNapgRbbxJfPdh7osQOXIK4CLvbVMB0UIr2OyzHhhsnqpRTRyN8IXFqZY6Ni4VDekGS4EuTGK-rUOABncrKVpnZb4CWk9GsJ90_FvrGZA4ld4QlbubRhgF4kh_uuvTc97rVeDPrIz1C0AQtokf_ZA5ol6My_37Y), [The Marketing Cost of Missed Appointment Calls in the Healthcare In…](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6996f342ca9f6bb46fc273bb/69e4f2a32188cf5b7973775a_621ef2f46891ea5f131e6fe4_The-Marketing-Cost-of-Missed-Calls-in-Healthcare-Industry.png)). When polyclinic receptionists miss calls, generic callback attempts often turn into frustrating telephone tag because the receptionist does not know which specialist the patient needs or which doctor's roster to consult.

An automated department routing script allows the patient to identify their required department immediately. This streamlines intake and ensures the inquiry connects directly to the relevant doctor's schedule.

### Multi-Speciality Routing Script Template

"Namaste, we missed your call at \[Clinic / Polyclinic Name\]. To help us direct you to the correct specialist department, please tap your required department below to view doctor timings and book your OPD visit: 1. Orthopaedics: \[Link 1\] 2. Dermatology & Cosmetology: \[Link 2\] 3. Gynaecology & Obstetrics: \[Link 3\] 4. Paediatrics & Vaccinations: \[Link 4\] 5. General Medicine: \[Link 5\]. For diagnostic lab tests or report pickups, reply 'LAB'. Our front desk coordinator will receive your selection directly."

### Implementation Notes: Multi-Speciality Routing

- Limit options to your top four to five clinical departments to prevent decision fatigue.
- If using WhatsApp, deploy interactive buttons or numeric menu replies ('Reply 1 for Ortho') to simplify input on mobile devices.
- Route department selections directly into the individual doctor's live calendar to avoid manual re-entry by the receptionist.

## Script 5: DPDP-Compliant Caller Verification and Consent Script

Patient data governance in India is governed by the [Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=) ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)). Under the DPDP Act, medical clinics are classified as Data Fiduciaries, while patients are Data Principals ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)). This statutory framework applies to all digital personal data processing across India, encompassing large hospital systems as well as solo general practitioners maintaining digital appointment registers ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)). Penalties for non-compliance can reach up to ₹250 crore for significant violations ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)).

A fundamental mandate of the DPDP Act is that consent must be clear, specific, informed, freely given, and revocable, written in plain language that the patient understands; verbal consent is considered insufficient ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)). Furthermore, under the principle of purpose limitation, a phone number collected solely for appointment booking or care coordination cannot be repurposed for promotional broadcasts or marketing offers without separate, explicit consent ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)). WhatsApp interactions similarly require transparent consent mechanisms before gathering personal details ([WhatsApp Surveys in India: Increase Survey Response Rates](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQFRZU_6j8ISKPJ1u8bGgWAg6oc9E0srWmNUag5a6kwpZpFiW5e_4lGftK2Eliu7QcKIaJcratvg6X8lIxRIvJadkko4WoUysVJto6m3pV6_QFfFfxHZnzWuRfQPSUKNQ0IWOBDO33a_sZMbEeuGQg==)). The text-back script below collects scheduling details while embedding explicit privacy compliance.

### DPDP-Compliant Consent and Intake Template

"Hello from \[Clinic Name\]. We missed your call and would be pleased to assist you. To reserve your consultation slot and register your visit, please provide your details securely here: \[Intake Link\]. By proceeding, you consent to \[Clinic Name\] processing your contact number and visit details solely for appointment scheduling, clinical reminders, and visit coordination in compliance with the DPDP Act 2023. We do not share your health data with third parties or send unsolicited promotional messages. You may revoke consent at any time by replying 'STOP'."

### Implementation Notes: DPDP Consent & Privacy

- Ensure the link routes to an intake page hosted in secure domestic cloud infrastructure without unconsented third-party trackers.
- Strictly isolate the phone numbers collected via this template from promotional marketing or broadcast lists unless separate opt-in consent is recorded ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)).
- Ensure patients have a straightforward way to revoke consent, as consent under the DPDP Act must be revocable ([DPDP Act 2023: Patient Data Compliance for Indian Doctors](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHWgQWMGfp7aWZEXXSICf6frY08ob0GZ1ZvhRTXeDh73vgK62XoiMgxRfTA93aMYTDSWaARWJP6c80hdul3LjOKGo6wsLCFwIyWlgOxoqWSmNUkY6UM7yyCyKrOdU1ehTtBsZjgrXJEu0T71zgqKzQ4tHnlX33ZcEw90I-lmHOYNkXvQdvNZVo=)).

## Script 6: The Abandoned Booking Nudge Sequence

Across the healthcare and social assistance sector, click-through rates (CTR) on links sent via text messaging average between 10% and 18% ([SMS Benchmarks for Healthcare and Social Assistance](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHq6fFZf0ckh2Aan8Cuh5MHGKjD2qzAxF-_yYLTh34dkjC8gIwPbxVXu5ew_VL5mKBL-zfs19rawdcs1D41OZRiziRe62Czk5NEkX4gNwUQyzukyFw19Kp08gGMGk2QEX79cm_9p3zw4wA6ls6rAI08M8or6mv5DSvSKJE8ML8Alfg4XSph2O7yUpcI)). This means that while a notable portion of callers click the appointment link sent in an initial text-back, some abandon the process midway. A patient might click the link, get distracted by an incoming call, hesitate over a specific doctor's consultation fee, or find that their preferred time slot is unavailable.

Because these patients have already demonstrated active intent by dialling your practice and opening your link, a polite, timed follow-up nudge recovers high-intent appointments that would otherwise lapse. Indian patient communication etiquette requires a helpful, non-intrusive tone rather than aggressive sales tactics.

### Abandoned Booking Follow-Up Script Template

"Namaste \[Patient Name / Caller\], we noticed you started booking a consultation at \[Clinic Name\] earlier today but did not finish selecting your slot. Did you encounter any difficulty with available doctor timings or procedure options? You can complete your slot selection here: \[Booking Link\]. Alternatively, if you would prefer our front desk team to arrange an alternative time or answer your questions personally, reply 'ASSIST' and we will call you back shortly during OPD hours."

### Implementation Notes: Abandoned Booking Follow-Up

- Send this follow-up between 20 and 45 minutes after the initial booking link was opened without confirmation.
- Send only one nudge message. Sending multiple automated reminders within a short period is viewed as spam and risks patient complaints under messaging regulations.
- Respect clinic operational hours: do not send automated nudge messages after 9:00 PM IST.

## Choosing Between Missed Call Text Back Script Templates and Automated Recovery

Implementing missed call text-back scripts can take two operational paths: manual execution or automated recovery systems. In a manual workflow, front-desk staff keep these templates saved in their mobile clipboards or desktop messaging portals. When the receptionist notices a missed call log on the clinic SIM or desk receiver, they manually paste and dispatch the relevant template. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, clinics must obtain clear, revocable consent for processing patient details for scheduling and cannot repurpose phone numbers for marketing without explicit consent.
