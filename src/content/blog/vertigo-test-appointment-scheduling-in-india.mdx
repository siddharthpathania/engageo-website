Because this stimulation induces temporary nystagmus, patients often feel brief dizziness or nausea during the procedure.

In busy Indian ENT practices, front desks handle high-volume OPD consultation queues where walk-ins and follow-up reviews arrive continuously. When lengthy balance assessments are mixed into open consultation streams, bottlenecks quickly emerge across the clinic:

- Overlapping Clinical Demands: If an acute vertigo patient feels nauseous or needs extra recovery time between positions, the clinician is delayed. This creates long wait times for routine OPD consultations.
- Queue Delays: Mixing short ear-nose-throat checkups with extended vestibular tests frustrates patients. Ambulatory patients wait too long, while dizzy patients endure noisy waiting areas.
- Technician Bottlenecks: Audiologists and technicians cannot leave mid-test to register walk-ins or answer reception queries. Dedicated staff availability remains the primary operational constraint.

To prevent these delays, clinics must separate diagnostic appointments from routine outpatient queues. Practices protect their workflow by setting fixed diagnostic blocks, such as reserved morning or afternoon sessions. Establishing rigid 90-minute booking rules in the scheduling calendar prevents front-desk staff from double-booking quick consultations over complex evaluations.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a standard videonystagmography (VNG) evaluation take?

A standard VNG evaluation takes approximately 60 to 90 minutes to administer. Because it requires ocular-motor testing, positional testing, and caloric stimulation, clinics typically schedule it in dedicated diagnostic blocks.

### Why do patients need 48-hour medication washouts before a vertigo test?

Certain medications such as sedatives, narcotics, and antidepressants can alter ocular reflexes and produce false diagnostic results during VNG testing. Suspending these unapproved drugs for 48 hours ensures accurate findings.

### Which medications should patients continue taking before vestibular testing?

Patients should continue taking vital maintenance therapies for conditions such as high blood pressure, heart disease, diabetes, and seizures.

### How does automated WhatsApp messaging reduce balance test cancellations?

Automated WhatsApp sequences deliver medication suspension alerts at 48 hours, confirmation prompts at 24 hours, and fasting reminders at 3 hours before the test, keeping patients prepared at each stage.
