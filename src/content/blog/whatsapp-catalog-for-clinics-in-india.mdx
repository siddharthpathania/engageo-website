Morning OPD hours in an Indian clinic are always hectic. Front desk staff must handle walk-in registrations, paper files, and ringing desk phones all at once. At the same time, the clinic phone buzzes with incoming chat messages. Patients ask the same questions every day: What are the consultation charges? Do you offer a preventive health check-up? What are the doctor timings today?

Answering these messages by hand takes up valuable time. It also keeps receptionists away from patients standing right in front of them. Today, patients in India want quick answers. They look for clear consultation fees and specialist details on messaging apps before they book a visit. If a clinic takes hours to reply, the patient often contacts another doctor nearby.

Businesses that use the WhatsApp Business app can set up a catalog on their profile to showcase products and services ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). For an Indian outpatient clinic, this feature provides a neat way to present OPD consultations and health packages. It works like a simple digital front desk. It answers common fee questions right away, sets clear expectations, and saves hours of desk work each week.

## Technical Setup for Digital Service Menus

Creating a catalog on the WhatsApp Business app lets a business [display its services directly on its profile](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US) ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). This setup is much better than sending long text messages or PDF rate cards. Mobile screens make PDFs hard to read. A neat catalog presents clinical services as clean, easy cards that patients can review in seconds.

Every item added to a WhatsApp catalog must include three core elements: a unique title, the country of origin, and at least one image showing the item ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). For medical centres, these basic requirements ensure each entry is clear and simple to identify:

- A unique title for each product or service ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). Clinics can use this field to name each specific doctor consultation or diagnostic panel clearly.
- At least one image displaying the product or service ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). Practices can upload a photo of the clinic reception or an informative graphic.
- The country of origin for the listed service ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)).

You can also add optional details to catalog listings. These include extra images, videos, written descriptions, website links, item codes, sale prices, and standard prices where pricing is supported ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). Clinics can use these extra fields to explain what is included in a visit. They can also add internal billing codes and web links that take patients to official clinic pages.

Larger centres can also link an existing Meta catalog directly to their WhatsApp Business app ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). This option is helpful for hospital chains that already manage service lists across Meta tools. Once published, businesses can share entire catalogs or single service cards with patients in chats, and patients can share them with family members on WhatsApp ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)).

## NMC Compliance Rules for Displaying Clinical Fees

When Indian clinics publish fees and services, they must follow rules set by the [National Medical Commission](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules) (NMC) ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)). The NMC lays down strict standards on ethical advertising and public communication for registered doctors and hospitals ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)).

Under NMC rules, registered medical practitioners are permitted to make a formal public declaration of their charges ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)). Doctors are also permitted to make formal announcements about starting practice, changing practice type or address, resuming work, or succeeding another doctor ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)). Sharing standard consultation charges helps provide price transparency to visiting patients without crossing ethical boundaries.

At the same time, the NMC strictly bans self-promotional ads and commercial solicitation ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)). When writing service cards, clinics must avoid several prohibited practices:

- Superlative claims: Listings must never use claims like 'best doctors', 'guaranteed cures', 'No.1 Specialist', 'painless treatment', or '100% success' ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)).
- Commercial discounts: Doctors and medical centres cannot offer discounts, limited-period deals, coupons, cashbacks, gifts, contests, or free procedures that could encourage unnecessary visits or solicit patients ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)).
- Paid reviews: NMC guidelines prohibit doctors and hospitals from buying, soliciting, or sharing fake, paid, or misleading patient reviews, ratings, testimonials, or influencer endorsements ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)).
- Treatment results: Healthcare rules ban before-and-after photos, advertising surgical outcomes, publishing clinical cases for promotional reasons, and running AI-generated ad campaigns ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)).

For practice owners, staying compliant is straightforward. Keep your service cards factual, neutral, and educational. Use the space as a professional directory of consultation types and clear fees, not as a commercial sales channel.

## Structuring Specialist Consultation Tiers and Health Packages

A good service menu arranges clinical care into simple groups. Patients can then find what they need without confusion. Specialist clinics should avoid listing hundreds of individual items. Instead, focus on clear consultation tiers and standard check-up packages.

### 1\. Specialist Consultation Tiers

WhatsApp catalog items support a unique title, a description, and a price field where supported ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). At the same time, NMC rules permit doctors to make a formal public declaration of their charges ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)). Rather than listing an unclear 'Doctor Visit', clinics can create distinct, informative cards:

- Initial Specialist OPD Consultation: Lists standard consultation charges, clinical specialty, and estimated visit duration.
- Follow-up Review Visit: States standard review charges applicable within a set validity period after an initial visit.
- Pre-Procedure Clinical Assessment: Outlines the evaluation visit required before minor procedures, explaining what the assessment involves.

### 2\. Objective Preventive Health Packages

Preventive check-ups are popular with families across India. When listing these packages, stay neutral and clear. Avoid marketing taglines or commercial sales claims, keeping communication in line with NMC rules against patient inducement ([NMC bans self-promotional ads by doctors and hospitals; rolls out s…](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2024/oct/07/nmc-bans-self-promotional-ads-by-doctors-and-hospitals-rolls-out-strict-rules)). Use factual names such as 'Diabetic Care Panel' or 'Senior Citizen Health Review'.

Catalog items can optionally feature text descriptions ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). Clinics can use this space to list the exact tests and services included in each package:

- Doctor consultations included in the check-up.
- Specific blood tests and diagnostic investigations covered in the package.
- Preparation guidelines, such as required fasting hours before blood collection.
- Estimated report delivery times and schedule for the follow-up doctor review.

## Connecting Inquiries to Live Appointment Scheduling

Listing compliant services solves the first part of patient communication. However, clinics often face a drop-off right after a patient views an item. When browsing a service card, patients usually send a chat message asking for an open slot. If that message arrives during peak clinic hours, the receptionist may not see it for hours.

A slow reply during OPD rush hours often means the patient looks for another clinic. Relying on manual chat replies slows down the desk and leads to lost bookings when staff are busy.

Catalog listings can optionally include website links and descriptions ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). Clinics can use these link fields to insert direct online booking URLs into each card. When a patient opens a card, they can tap the link to view open calendar slots, select a time, and confirm their visit.

Connecting booking links to your calendar or clinic software keeps slot availability accurate in real time. The front desk avoids double-booking errors, and the patient gets an instant confirmation on WhatsApp without long back-and-forth messaging.

## Comparing Options: Static Menus Versus Automated Recovery

Clinic owners need to decide how WhatsApp fits into their daily patient flow. Depending on patient volume and front-desk staffing, practices usually consider two main paths.

### Option 1: Standalone WhatsApp Business Catalog

A standard catalog in the WhatsApp Business app works well for small, single-doctor clinics with light daily inquiry volumes. Businesses can share catalogs and specific service items with patients over WhatsApp ([About catalog | WhatsApp Help Center](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/?locale=en_US)). Front desk staff can send these cards to answer patient questions during normal office hours.

However, this basic approach has limitations. It requires patients to save your number and text you first. More importantly, it does not solve missed phone calls. When prospective patients call during peak hours or after closing and get no answer, a static catalog cannot help them.

### Option 2: Integrated Missed Call Recovery and Messaging Automation

For busy OPDs and specialist practices in fields like dental, dermatology, IVF, and orthopaedics, phone calls remain the main booking channel. In these busy practices, connecting missed call handling with automated WhatsApp messaging creates a dependable workflow.

When the front desk cannot pick up an incoming call, an automated recovery system steps in. The caller immediately receives a WhatsApp message with direct links to the clinic service menu and an appointment booking page. This turns a missed call into a booked visit while keeping the receptionist focused on patients in the clinic.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Indian doctors legally publish consultation charges on a WhatsApp catalog?

Yes. National Medical Commission rules allow registered medical practitioners to make a formal public declaration of their consultation charges.

### What information must each item in a WhatsApp catalog contain?

Every catalog item requires a unique title, the country of origin, and at least one image showing the service or product.

### Are healthcare discounts or promotional claims permitted on clinic service cards?

No. NMC guidelines strictly prohibit discounts, promotional offers, coupons, free procedures, and superlative claims like guaranteed cures or top doctor titles.

### How can clinics connect catalog inquiries directly to appointment booking?

Clinics can add direct booking links in the optional link field of catalog cards, letting patients view available calendar slots and confirm appointments instantly.
